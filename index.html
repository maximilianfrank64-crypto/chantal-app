What Chantal is

Chantal runs on a private home server and is used only by its operator. It is not publicly accessible and has no other users or sign-ups.

Google services used

  • Gmail: reads emails and creates drafts (never sends or deletes).
  • Google Calendar: reads events and creates new events.
  • YouTube YouTube API Services: uploads the operator's own videos to his own YouTube channel "Grenzenlos" (@grenzenlos_DOKU) as private, sets title, description, tags, thumbnail and captions.

Chantal uses YouTube API Services. By using Chantal you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
