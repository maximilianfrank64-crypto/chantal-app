Privacy Policy – Chantal

Last updated: October 2026
Responsible: Maximilian Frank, maximilian.frank64@gmail.com

1. What Chantal is

Chantal is a private, self-hosted assistant. It runs only on the operator's private home server and is used only by the operator. There are no public sign-ups and no other user accounts.

2. YouTube API Services

Chantal uses YouTube API Services to upload the operator's own videos to his own YouTube channel. By using Chantal, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

YouTube data accessed: only what is needed to upload videos to the operator's own channel (video file, title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions) and the ID of the uploaded video. Chantal does not read, collect or analyse data of other YouTube users or channels.

3. Other Google data accessed

  • Gmail: reading emails and creating drafts. Chantal never sends or deletes emails.
  • Google Calendar: reading events and creating new events.

4. How the data is used

Summarising incoming messages, detecting appointments and adding them to the calendar, preparing reply drafts, and uploading the operator's own videos to his own YouTube channel as private videos.

5. Storage

All processing happens on the operator's private server. The only stored YouTube data is the ID of each uploaded video, kept in a local table to track production status. OAuth access tokens are stored encrypted on the same server. Email and calendar contents are not stored permanently in an external database. Text content is sent for processing to language-model providers (Anthropic, OpenAI) and to ElevenLabs for voice-over.

6. Cookies and device information

Chantal has no public website or app for users. It does not place cookies and does not store or access information on users' devices.

7. Sharing

No data is shared with third parties for advertising or analytics. Data is only passed to the processors named in section 5, and to Google/YouTube when uploading.

8. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (or myaccount.google.com/permissions). After revocation, the stored access tokens become invalid and are deleted. Stored YouTube video IDs are deleted on request within 7 days; send an email to maximilian.frank64@gmail.com. Data obtained through the YouTube API is not kept longer than 30 days unless it is refreshed through the API.
